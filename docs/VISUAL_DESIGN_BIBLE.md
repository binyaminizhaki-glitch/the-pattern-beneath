# The Pattern Beneath — visual direction

## World and medium

Vesper, a fictional New England town in 1892. Cinematic painterly realism: tactile wood, worn paper, dark wool, autumn leaves, handwritten work. Original generated environment paintings and portraits are the principal art; the small live figures and connections remain a readable simulation layer over the town panorama.

## Light, color, type

Light enters from the left. Warm amber highlights meet cool window light and deep charcoal/forest shadows. Ochre wallpaper (#a17a37), parchment (#efe3c6), forest (#23372e), ink (#171c18), brass (#c7a76a). Never bleach the artwork. Georgia supplies literary display/body dialogue; Gill Sans/Calibri/system sans supplies concise controls. Light text sits on sufficiently opaque dark scrims, printed newspaper text on warm paper.

## Composition

- Opening: bedroom fills the viewport. Window/desk left, title over the dark wallpaper right. Only title, one sentence, and Enter; small persistent sound/help controls.
- Gazette: a real printing desk; the paper carries the actual initial headline and counts. An inspectable editorial phrase reveals an inference, not a new fact.
- Clara: large, intimate portrait on the left, short dialogue on the right. On phones, portrait becomes the top third with a readable dialogue stage below.
- Institutions: distinct school, clinic and print-house environments. Inspecting a rule exposes its living representative and recorded policy.
- Town: richly lit panorama with live citizens, real social ties, access visits and publications. Compact strategy controls occupy the right quarter, then recede during consequences.
- Turning point: manuscript in window light. Questions arise from the actual record.
- Comparison: synchronized versions of the same panorama; Clara's same portrait accompanies both recorded lives. No visual architectural destruction or success is invented.
- Revelation: peeled wallpaper exposes the actual causal chain. Return to the original bedroom, now understood differently.

## Characters

Clara Vale (citizen-0): auburn pinned hair, green eyes, freckles, forest dress, cream collar, manuscript. Henry Bell (clinic leader): thoughtful middle-aged physician, spectacles, beard, charcoal waistcoat; believes rules can protect. Benjamin Grey (Gazette leader): clean-shaven editor, dark wavy hair, brown waistcoat, ink-stained hands; wants a legible public account. Reuse each exact portrait across every scene and inspector. Other citizens retain individualized simulation figures; never reuse a named portrait for them.

## Motion and sound

Slow room camera and sparse dust; page-like scene arrivals; portraits hold still enough to read. A message moves toward its actual messenger, along logged exposure/ties, into beliefs, rules, opportunities, work, then news. The control surface recedes during the 4.2-second sequence. Skip always available. Reduced motion removes decorative movement. Wallpaper separates at the final reveal; text remains immediately accessible. Optional locally synthesized quiet wind/press ambience, muted by default, explicit sound toggle; no spoken or essential audio content.

## Asset policy

Eleven original images generated with the built-in OpenAI image generation tool on 2026-10-25. Local optimized WebP files, no network image/font dependencies. Original provenance and usage recorded in public/art/manifest.json. Illustrative art is fictional; all overlaid outcomes, policies and dialogue circumstances come from immutable simulation records. No image is historical evidence.
