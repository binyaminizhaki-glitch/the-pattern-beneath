# Explicit model assumptions

48 adults: 24 pairs, each pair given identical immutable aptitude in [0.45, 0.95]. Gender, peer graph (four bidirectional neighbours per person), authority trust, susceptibility and resilience are fixed. Equal pair aptitude is an experimental control in fiction, not an empirical statement about populations. Initial beliefs and institutional biases encode an already restrictive town. Week zero's opening observation is generated by the same decision mechanism as later weeks.

All noise is a stateless hash of seed, turn, citizen ID and event type. The played and passive runs share the exact school, clinic, writing and editorial draws. No random sequence can shift when an intervention changes the number of manuscripts.

Beliefs update synchronously from previous peers, a relevant institution leader, prior news and the selected message. Weights: peer correction .18, authority correction .10×trust, message .48×exposure×susceptibility, news .065×(.5+.5×trust). Strong opposing prior belief reduces message uptake to .62. Restricted women contribute a small negative testimony term (.025×resilience); this is a stylized feedback from lived institutional experience. Physicians reach high-trust citizens and leaders; Gazette reaches 90% with moderate credibility; community reaches a rotating local third with occasional school-leader contact. Peers carry local effects into later weeks. These coefficients were selected for legible eight-week demonstrations, not fitted to evidence.

Policy bias retains 75% of its last value and moves 25% toward .65×leader + .35×population belief. Rules use hysteresis (.38 restricted/.30 exit; .12 open/.20 exit). Probabilities use continuous bias; policy labels summarize without discrete flicker.

Seminar admission = clamp(.83 + .08×(aptitude−.7) − .74×positive school bias for women). Writing support = clamp(.94 − .86×positive clinic bias for women). Clinical support affects writing time/agency only. Agency retains 78% and responds to supported time, plus a small resilience term. Seminar access adds acquired training, separately stored from fixed aptitude.

At least one of seminar access or supported writing time is required for a manuscript. Completion probability depends on agency, that week's seminar, acquired training and internalized restrictive belief. Editorial acceptance depends on fixed aptitude, training and gatekeeping. Men face the common thresholds; women face the additional fictional policy restrictions. Record every threshold and draw, including failed manuscripts. A restrictive rule can lower a probability without being the sole cause of a particular rejection; logs must not claim deterministic individual but-for causation without comparison.

Metrics derive from individual records: endorsement = belief > .20; restriction degree = mean positive policy bias; opportunity display = seminar admissions by gender; publication share = actual accepted manuscripts, with null when none. Cumulative counts include the explicitly labeled opening edition (week zero). Final intervention-window comparisons additionally subtract week zero.

The Gazette counts publications, then its editor interprets the gap. Fewer than five outputs or a gap below two produces a cautious frame. A hierarchy-endorsing editor may treat a male advantage as natural; a critical editor questions the access process. The resulting signal is used next week, never retrospectively in the current belief update.

Snapshots, agents, metrics, messages and records are frozen. Functions return new state. Eight turns stop further advances. All observations are fictional model outputs; no diagnostic or physical mechanisms change. Counterfactual means the same fictional world without player messages, not historical truth.
