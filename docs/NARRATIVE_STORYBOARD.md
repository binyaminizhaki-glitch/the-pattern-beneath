# Scene architecture and storyboard

The pure simulation is authoritative and unchanged. `sceneReducer` owns nine explicit scene kinds; dialogue questions and object inspections never call the engine. See VISUAL_DESIGN_BIBLE.md for art direction.

1. TITLE: a richly painted bedroom, window light, writing desk, wallpaper and closed door. Minimal title and Enter the Story.
2. PROLOGUE: actual week-zero Gazette counts and editorial inference; then Clara's actual manuscript/access situation. Seed 42 admits her to a seminar but withholds clinic support and declines her completed manuscript. Do not replace this with a scripted seminar rejection.
3. CHARACTER_ENCOUNTER: Clara introduces her ambition. Branches examine hopes, rules and work; all situation and outcome lines derive from records.
4. TOWN_GAMEPLAY: Act I (weeks 0–2), Act II (3–5), Act III (6–8). Citizens and leaders can be encountered through inspectors.
5. SIMULATION_CONSEQUENCE: real 4.2-second, seven-phase event projection, skippable and locked against duplicate release. Then record the act's next scene.
6. STORY_TURNING_POINT: after week four, archive/wallpaper imagery questions what the town has measured. Its language adapts to restrictive policy degree.
7. Recurring CHARACTER_ENCOUNTER: Clara after weeks 2, 5 and 7; physician Henry Bell after weeks 1 and 6; editor Benjamin Grey after week 3. Their exact portraits recur in profiles. Beliefs, institutional rules and recorded outputs determine dialogue; static ambitions supply perspective. No guaranteed optimistic outcome.
8. COUNTERFACTUAL_REVEAL after week eight: synchronized worlds, Clara pinned in both; follow her two real paths; reveal unchanged aptitude. Detailed records stay optional.
9. PATTERN_REVEAL: animated wallpaper separates to show narrative, belief, authority, rules, opportunity, behavior, measured outcomes and interpreted evidence returning to belief.
10. LITERARY_EPILOGUE: return to the exact opening bedroom. John and missing testimony, one brief verified fragment, interpretive limits. “The pattern was invented. The consequences were real.” Replay, another possibility, about the experiment and review remain available.

All scenes occupy 100dvh; only optional evidence overlays scroll internally. Camera motion, page arrivals and the wallpaper lift respect reduced-motion preferences. Environmental movement is illustrative, not an added simulated outcome. Ambience is locally synthesized and muted by default. The no-intervention comparison does not claim a divergence when the two rules match.
