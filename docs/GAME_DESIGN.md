# Game design

The final presentation follows [the narrative storyboard](NARRATIVE_STORYBOARD.md). All eight explicit scene types occupy a fixed viewport. Clara recurs after weeks 2, 5 and 7; the archive is a full scene after week 4; the final comparison leads to a literary epilogue. Dialogue reveals context and never invokes the engine. Optional evidence and sources use contained overlays. Desktop town play occupies roughly 75% of the stage; mobile has a compact lower HUD and comparison tabs. This supersedes the original scrollable editorial layout.

One broadcast each week for eight weeks. Choose natural hierarchy or equal potential, then Physicians' Council, Town Gazette or Community Circle. No win score. An optional quiet week allows inspection of the passive model.

The cold open's bulletin reports actual simulated pre-game publications; its interpretation is explicitly editorial. A briefing explains controls. At week four an archival note questions whether opportunity and aptitude have been distinguished. At week eight the same-seed passive world, unchanged matched aptitude, citizen divergence and literary return become available.

Town tokens are persistent citizens. Selecting one reveals belief, agency, network and actual weekly school/clinic/writing/publication decisions. Institutional controls reveal policy inertia and leaders. Historical weeks are read-only. The report always labels its time window and denominators.

No external fonts, media, requests or accounts. Refresh resets the experiment; this is stated before play. The source panel is accessible throughout. Dialogs use native focus trapping, Escape and focus restoration.
